What I do

I work on products with many moving parts and little room for ambiguity. I’m often involved before the team has settled on what something is, what it should be called, or how people should move through it. I help establish product structure, then write the interface and carry the work through research, review, and launch.

I’ve done this across conversational AI, advertiser and creator tools, Prime Video, and global payment experiences, working closely with product, design, research, science, engineering, legal, and privacy.

I’m now exploring hands-on Staff and Principal content design roles where I can bring that experience to shaping products and content systems while staying close to the writing.
