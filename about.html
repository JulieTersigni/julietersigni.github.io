How I work

Four habits

  1. Structure before sentences

    I start with the content model, information architecture, and taxonomy. Naming and hierarchy decide whether the copy has to work hard later, so that’s where I’d rather spend the argument.

  2. Design in the constraint

    A remote control, a living room, live sport, a regulated checkout. The constraint is the brief — I write to what the surface can actually support rather than to an ideal.

  3. Bring partners along early

    Legal, privacy, engineering, science, and PM aren’t reviewers at the end. Involving them while the language is still soft is how content decisions survive to launch.

  4. Prove it, then reuse it

    Test with users, measure what changed, and turn the result into a pattern other teams can pick up. The best outcome is a decision nobody has to make twice.
