About me

My work spans conversational AI, advertising, streaming, commerce, and global payments. At Amazon, I shape advertiser and customer experiences across Ads and Prime Video. Before that, I led content design for Mastercard’s global payment products.

I work on products with many moving parts and little room for ambiguity. I’m often involved before a team has settled on what something is, what it should be called, or how people should move through it. I help establish the product structure, write the interface, and carry the work through research, review, and launch.

That has included defining interaction models and evaluation criteria for conversational AI, building taxonomies for viewer feedback, translating behavioral signals into recommendations for advertisers, and making global payment flows easier to understand and recover from.

I work closely with product, design, research, science, engineering, legal, and privacy. At Mastercard, I also managed and mentored a content designer, established an unmoderated research practice, and led workshops, critiques, and executive presentations.

I’m exploring hands-on Staff and Principal content design roles where I can help shape the product and stay close to the writing.

How I work

Four habits

  1. Understand the product and the decision

    Before I write, I make sure I understand what the product does, what the customer is trying to accomplish, and what information they need.

  2. Design for the actual context

    I account for how and where someone will use the product—whether they’re navigating with a remote, watching a live game, building an ad campaign, or trying to complete a payment.

  3. Bring the right people in early

    I bring in product, design, research, engineering, science, legal, and privacy early enough for the findings to change the product.

  4. Test, measure, and make it reusable

    I use research and experiment data to challenge assumptions, then turn what works into guidance and patterns other teams can reuse.
